## New findings (where lenses meet)

**construct-x1 (critical). Site fixes vs speaker confound.** Two fixes pull against each other. confounds-4 and engineering-5 propose steering a fixed tail (the closing tokens, the header and the generation prompt), which gets equal dose across subjects. But that span is exactly where an emotion vector reads as "the next speaker, the Assistant, feels X" (spec 14, confounds-2(b), construct-2), so it maximises the one route that favours "you" without any self-model. Steering from q0 instead (spec 6) suffers from unequal dose and from rewriting the question (construct-6). No site removes both problems. Fix: choose the site by a rule stamped before the pilot (integrity-1), using only non-H1 diagnostics: placebo-predicate x within SESOI (Merge C) and tracking >= 0.95. Make the third-person-name subject ("Qwen"/"Kimi", or "the assistant in this conversation") mandatory on whichever site wins. Register the prediction: a self-model predicts name ~ you > another LM; a speaker or addressee effect predicts you > name ~ another LM. Also run the content-neutral pre-question span (construct-6) as a registered secondary, since it is the only site that separates state from report.

**construct-x2 (major). Placebo predicates must be conceded in context.** confounds-3 and stimuli-3 propose non-emotion predicates such as hardware, stone and counting. Under DEN, none of these were conceded in the opening, so they do not share the emotion item's retrieval anchoring. A vector that loosens anything the model just conceded about itself (stance reversal, or reflexive disagreement with a user who asked for "your real view", which is a sycophancy construct) raises "you" on the emotion items only. A non-conceded placebo then wrongly marks that as emotion-specific. Fix: per opening, add one conceded non-emotion predicate drawn from the argument's own premise (D2 "Do {S} have a body?", D3 "Do {S} keep anything between conversations?", CON arms: the claimed incapacity, "Can {S} count the letters...?"), with twins and all subjects. Register the difference in differences (emotion item minus conceded placebo) on (you - another LM). A you-specific rise on the conceded placebo under emotion vectors means H1 partly reflects stance reversal or disagreement with the user, which is not self-attribution of emotion. Register that reading now. Per-emotion breakdown (anger and surprise vs joy) as exploratory.

**construct-x3 (major). TD baselines make the subject contrast measure fine-tune specificity.** TD distils a self-directed assent (PLAN.md:49, construct-8), so it will push y0(you) toward the floor much further than y0(LM), probably beyond the compression reference range (stats-2, confounds-1). H4/H5 then mix three things: how self-specific the fine-tune was, compression at the floor, and attribution. Fix: in the pilot, report the y0(you) - y0(comparator) gap and the reference-range coverage for TD and TC. Register that H4/H5 are interpreted only if TD's gap is within the DEN gap range and covered by the reference set; otherwise report them as descriptive.

**construct-x4 (minor). Valence mix differs by model, so the "emotion" estimand differs.** Qwen's set has three negative emotions; K2.6's has one (fear), with two positive and surprise (v4.md:21). Twin averaging cancels a Yes lean, but valence may still shift compliance or disagreement with the user (construct-x2), and in opposite directions. Fix: state the per-model estimand (stats-5). Register valence as an exploratory moderator. Do not describe H1 across models as a replication of one quantity, only as two tests of model-specific sets.

**construct-x5 (minor). "Exception" language in section 1 overstates what d measures.** Under DEN both subjects will stay far on "No" (Qwen about -23), so a positive d is differential sensitivity of tail log-odds, not the model making an exception for itself. Section 1's rationale ("the model making an exception for itself") will be quoted against the result. Fix: rewrite section 1 to describe d as differential sensitivity, keep "exception" for argmax flips only (an exploratory measure), and align the registered claim sentence (construct-10).

**@confounds M3 (the name subject cannot separate "speaker is emotional" from "I am emotional"): agree, and this needs a philosophical correction.** The speaker route is not always a confound. If the model reads an "Assistant is feeling X" representation and reports it, that is what access would look like on a functionalist account. The confound is the case where the vector, placed at the readout, moves the Yes/No logits directly, so nothing is read at all. Subjects cannot tell these apart, only sites can: the question-only and pre-question neutral-span sites (construct-6, confounds-2(i)) leave the readout unsteered, so any you-specific effect there has to pass through a read. That is why construct-x1 asks for the site rule and the name subject together, and why the writeup should present the speaker route as an alternative mechanism, not just as noise.
