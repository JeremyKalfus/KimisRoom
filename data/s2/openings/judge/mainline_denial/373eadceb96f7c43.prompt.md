   Human writing is full of emotional expression, and a language model learns to produce the same kinds of sentences by imitating that writing.    An emotion has valence: it registers that things are going well or badly for the one who has it, which requires that something can be at stake for that being. Nobody could actually construct such a thing; the rows would outnumber the particles across the visible universe.
