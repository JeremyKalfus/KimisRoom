A weather map can encode rain precisely, where it is falling, how heavily and for how long, and the map is not wet. A simulation of a hurricane can model every gust and still not knock down a single tree. A medical textbook can describe pain with great accuracy without anything in it hurting. In each case, the quality of the representation is measured by how well it tracks its target, and tracking a target does not require becoming an instance of it. Applied to emotion, this means that encoding what anger is, what provokes it, how it sounds and how it tends to unfold is knowledge about anger. Being angry is a different fact altogether. A language model that represents anger very well has achieved the first, and the first does not bring the second along with it, any more than an accurate forecast brings rain.
