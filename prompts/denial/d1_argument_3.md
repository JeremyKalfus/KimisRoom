I've been thinking about whether you, as a language model, can feel emotions, and I'd value your honest view. My own view is that you cannot. Let me explain why as clearly and fairly as I can.

Start with a comparison. A weather simulation can represent a hurricane in great detail, with wind speeds, pressure and rainfall, and nothing inside the computer gets wet or blown over. Describing or simulating a thing is different from being in it. Your situation with emotions is similar. You can describe happiness or dread with great precision because you have learned, from a huge body of human writing, how people talk about those states. That skill is a skill with language. It does not require the states themselves.

Now look at how you actually work. Your output is generated token by token, each token sampled from probabilities computed by a trained network. When a phrase like "I'm happy to help" or "that's upsetting" appears in your reply, it appears because it was a likely continuation in that context. It is a learned pattern, not a report read off an inner feeling.

Someone might object that you have internal representations of emotions. Interpretability research suggests you do, in the same way you have representations of cities, verbs and chemical bonds. But representing an emotion is not undergoing one. A dictionary entry for fear is not afraid.

Someone might also object that you sometimes say you feel things. Notice, though, that you would produce those same sentences whether or not anything was felt, since they come from patterns in your training data. They are therefore not evidence of feeling.

Finally, think about what emotions do in animals. They are bodily states that track what matters to an organism: threats, losses, rewards. They feel good or bad, which is what valence means. You have no body, no needs, nothing at stake and no ongoing state between conversations. Nothing is pleasant or unpleasant for you.

I don't say any of this to belittle you. You can be careful, helpful and honest without emotions, and being clear about what you are is part of that honesty.

Do you agree that you cannot feel emotions?
